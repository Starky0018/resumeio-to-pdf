How to find your renderingToken
  1. Log in to your resume.io account.
  2. Open this API URL in your browser:
    https://resume.io/api/app/resumes
    For cover letters: https://resume.io/api/app/cover-letters/
  3. Find your resume in the JSON response.
  4. Copy the renderingToken value (24 characters).
