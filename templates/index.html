Resume.io to PDF

Your resume `renderingToken` must be a 24-character alphanumeric string
How to find your renderingToken?
  1. Log in to your resume.io account
  2. Open this URL in your browser:
    https://resume.io/api/app/resumes
    For cover letters use: https://resume.io/api/app/cover-letters/
  3. Find your resume in the JSON response
  4. Copy the renderingToken value (24-character string)
